## SaMeiers/PopLib — upstream bug fixes (consumed as patches, not forked)

- <https://github.com/SaMeiers/PopLib> — **AGPL-3.0**
- Its `PORT-CHANGES.md` documents framework bugs that upstream (archived) never
  fixed. We consume the desktop-relevant subset as reviewable unified diffs in
  `port/patches/poplib/` (generated by `port/make-patches.py`, applied by
  `port/apply-patches.py`). This keeps `external/poplib` pinned to the pristine
  upstream commit without forking or vendoring the framework.
- Fixes consumed: bare XML attribute values, `MTRand` sign mask, signed kerning
  offsets, `SDL_FColor` 0–1 range, logical presentation at design resolution,
  and skipping the defunct `discordrpc` submodule. Handheld-only changes
  (libopenmpt, KMSDRM, software cursor, Android keyboard) are NOT included.
